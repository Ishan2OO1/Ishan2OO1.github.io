AAAI 2026 · HUMAN-CENTRIC MANUFACTURING WORKSHOP · 2025

Human-Centric AI for Sustainable Manufacturing

Real-time emission-aware decision support in photovoltaic recycling. Five models were compared — linear regression, a neural network, TabNet, Random Forest and XGBoost — and combined into a weighted ensemble, with XGBoost-based imputation filling missing data. The dataset was built from IEA-PVPS, openLCA and EPA sources, and scenario analysis showed operators the trade-offs between recovery, cost and emissions.

  • 11.8%ensemble error
  • 62.3%linear regression error
  • 0.97R-squared
  • 12recycling processes
